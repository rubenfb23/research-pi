# Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not promised dates. [Verification status](docs/status.md) distinguishes implemented behavior from scientific validation.

## 1.5: visible and reproducible research workflows

- Visual README, actual terminal capture, replayable recording, examples and community files.
- Twenty-run real-dataset study; explicit numeric binary CSV ingestion.
- Frozen custom Python method sources with measured predictions, ten seeds and traceable aggregation.
- DOI resolution and bibliographic metadata comparisons with retained sources.
- Diagnostics and a twenty-microtask benchmark runner for ResearchPi, Claude Code and Codex.

## Next: broader scientific execution

- Regression, grouped/temporal splits and nested selection without test leakage.
- Appropriate uncertainty, paired comparisons and explicit applicability protocols.
- Custom environments, dependency/input manifests and stronger process/evidence isolation.
- Real interrupted-run recovery benchmarks, LLM evaluations and compute budgets.

## Next: literature and publication workflows

- More attributed expert notes, updated venue profiles and task-specific research skills.
- Non-DOI bibliographic verification, corrections/retractions and source-to-claim review.
- LaTeX/BibTeX projects, reproducible figures and rendered print/layout checks.
- Causal estimators with explicit identification assumptions and sensitivity analysis.

## Next: reliability and comparative evidence

- Broader live provider/OS coverage and interactive clipboard/keyboard checks.
- Upstream dependency advisory resolution; signed Windows packages and macOS notarization.
- Held-out end-to-end research tasks, blinded scientific review and published repeated trials.

Propose concrete contributions through the research workflow issue form. Shared library notes need attribution, inspected sections, dates and clear scope.
