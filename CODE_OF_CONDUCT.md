# Community code of conduct

ResearchPi welcomes researchers and contributors of all backgrounds. Treat people respectfully, discuss evidence and technical decisions constructively, and acknowledge others' work. Explain disagreements without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact available on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/rubenfb23). Do not post private details in public reports. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants depending on severity and recurrence. Reports will be reviewed with attention to privacy and fairness. Maintainers are accountable to the same expectations.
